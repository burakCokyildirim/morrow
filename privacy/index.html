Privacy Policy

Morrow · Last updated: 18 September 2026

Morrow is a personal wishlist app that keeps the things you want in one calm list. This page explains what data the app touches — and what it never touches.

Morrow is the app's name in English; the same app appears as Belki in Turkish.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in. We don't create an identity for you.
  • Your saved items live on your device and in your own iCloud. We can't reach them.
  • No ads, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no third-party tracking, no affiliate links.
  • We don't send the links, titles, notes or prices you save to any server — we don't run one.

Where your items live

Everything you save (link, title, image, price, note, tags, lists) is stored in a local database on your device. If iCloud is on, it syncs between your own devices through Apple's private CloudKit database. That data sits inside your Apple account; as the developer we cannot read, download or delete it. If iCloud is off, the app quietly works in local-only mode and never pushes you to sign in.

What happens when you save a link

When you share or paste a link, the app reads that page: a request goes directly to that site's server to extract the title, image and price (including the site icon, again from the site's own address). The request does not pass through us; there is no server of ours in the middle. We cannot see which sites you browse — only the page you chose to save is read. Tracking parameters (such as utm_*) are stripped from the address we store.

Clipboard

We read a link from your clipboard only at the moment you tap to use it. The value is not stored or sent anywhere; if you don't save it, nothing remains.

Smart tagging

The feature that suggests category tags runs entirely on your device where the hardware supports it (Apple's on-device model). Your titles and links are not sent anywhere for this. You can turn it off in Settings.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled with RevenueCat. RevenueCat stores your purchase history (which product, when, whether it is active) under an anonymous identifier. We do not link that identifier to your name, email or anything else — we don't hold such data. Payment is processed by Apple; your card details never reach us or RevenueCat.

Analytics

This version ships with no analytics or measurement tool: we collect no usage data. If we add anonymous measurement later, this page will be updated and these limits will hold: full addresses, titles, notes and prices never enter any measurement, and no advertising identifier is used.

Notifications

The app does not ask for notification permission and does not send you reminders.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no age information.

Deleting your data

Because the data is yours, deletion is yours too: remove an item inside the app, or remove everything by deleting the app and clearing its iCloud data in Settings. Settings ▸ "Export your data" gives you a JSON copy of everything.

Changes to this page

Changes are published here and the date above is updated. An update that changes the app's data behaviour is also noted in the release notes.
